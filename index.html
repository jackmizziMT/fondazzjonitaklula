About our activities

Fondazzjoni kulturali b'għeruqha f'Għawdex

Fondazzjoni ta' Klula is a voluntary organisation based in Santa Luċija, Gozo. We safeguard the village's heritage, bring the community together through cultural programming, and share what makes this part of the island worth knowing, with everyone who's curious enough to look.

About the Fondazzjoni

European Destination of Excellence

Awarded to Santa Luċija by the European Commission in 2008, one of only eight destinations ever selected across Malta and Gozo.
